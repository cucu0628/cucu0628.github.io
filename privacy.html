Privacy

Last updated: 31 October 2026 · Applies to asked.hu and its subdomains (e.g. miner.asked.hu) · Magyarul

In short

  • I don't ask for your name, email address or any account.
  • Game saves are stored only in your own browser.
  • Some games show optional ads from Google, which uses cookies for them. You can opt out of personalised ads.

Game saves

The games (for example Deep Debt at miner.asked.hu) save your progress and settings in your browser's local storage (localStorage). This data stays on your device and is never sent to me. You can delete it at any time from the game's settings or by clearing the site data in your browser.

Ads

The web version of Deep Debt shows optional, rewarded ads through Google AdSense, only when you start one with a button. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and/or other sites on the Internet.

  • You can turn off personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of other vendors' cookies at aboutads.info.
  • How Google uses data from partner sites: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent by Google's consent message before any personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice later through the link the message provides.

Hosting and fonts

The sites are served by GitHub Pages, which may log technical data (such as IP addresses) for security purposes. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Details: GitHub Privacy Statement, Google Privacy Policy.

On other sites

If you play my games on another website (for example CrazyGames), the ads and data there are covered by that site's privacy policy.

Children

The sites and games do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Contact

If you have a question, open an issue in one of the repos on my GitHub profile, for example in deep-debt.
